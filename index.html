Everything is live. Dive in.

One instrument, three ways to play: a keyboard, a 16-track sequencer, and a bank of MIDI knobs. Every change happens while the music runs.

01 — Sequence

Sixteen tracks. Each with its own time.

One track per MIDI channel, up to 64 steps each. Run tracks at different lengths and speeds for polymeters and polyrhythms that never repeat the same way twice.

  • DRUM, MONO and POLY track types
  • Gate, velocity, accent, glide, tie and probability per step
  • Internal clock or external MIDI sync

02 — Generate

Let it write the bassline.

Built-in generators roll basslines, leads, arpeggios and harmonies that stay in your key and scale. Turn FILL, ACCENT and GLIDE until it grooves. Don't like it? Hold to undo.

  • Bass, Lead, Arp, Harm, Comp and Skank engines
  • Classic random or Euclidean rhythms
  • 18 drum styles with live fill and motion

03 — Arpeggiate

Nine ways to walk a chord.

Set any MONO track to ARP and it becomes an arpeggiator. Span the chord over up to four octaves, pick a walk order, then arm a sub-page and the whole encoder row turns into that page's controls.

  • Walks: up, down, up-down, random ×2, out-in, in-out, converge, diverge
  • Four sub-pages — ARP ORDER · ARP MOTION · ARP GENERATIVE · ARP OPTIONS
  • Markov and random-walk engines with seed, rate, depth and memory

    04 — Progress

    A progression engine with taste.

    In KEY 4 the instrument proposes chords from a real genre table: eighteen transition matrices, chord qualities, inversions and rhythm templates measured from the music itself. Tap to accept, hold to bake it into the sequence.

    • 18 genre grammars — Pop, Rock, Jazz, Funk, Reggae, Dubtechno, Latin, Modal…
    • Cadence bands: authentic · plagal · deceptive · half
    • Phrase templates FREE / AABB / ABAB / ABAC / AABA
    • Scale, root, octave and chord latch on the encoders

    05 — Follow

    Chain tracks into harmony.

    Point one track at another and it listens. A lead follows your chords, a harmony track reads your bassline, a bass locks to the progression. Change one part and the others move with it.

    • Bass → Harm → Chords → Lead chains
    • Follow, or freeze the chord of the moment
    • Chord-tone and guide-tone mapping

    HARM CH 2 · FOLLOW

    06 — Control

    Eight knobs. Your whole studio.

    Switch to encoder mode and the eight knobs become MIDI CC controllers, with banks for 36 CCs. Your DAW sends values back, so the knobs always match. Drive Ableton Live clips and devices too.

    • CC banks F1–F4
    • Two-way value feedback
    • Ableton Live clip and device control
